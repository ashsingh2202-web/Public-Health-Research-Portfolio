## 📂 Featured Research Projects

### Mpox Surveillance Analysis

**Methods:** Surveillance Epidemiology, Logistic Regression, Health Disparities Analysis

**Focus:** Investigated demographic patterns, hospitalization predictors, and disease burden among Florida Mpox cases.

🔗 Repository: [Mpox](https://github.com/ashsingh2202-web/Mpox)

---

### Malaria & Dengue Spatial Epidemiology

**Methods:** GIS Mapping, Spatial Analysis, Environmental Risk Assessment

**Focus:** Identified transmission hotspots and environmental predictors of vector-borne disease across study regions in India.

🔗 Repository: [Malaria-Dengue](https://github.com/ashsingh2202-web/Malaria-Dengue)

---

### Sickle Cell Disease: Home-Based Pain Management

**Methods:** Survey Research, Healthcare Utilization Analysis

**Focus:** Examined pain management strategies, self-efficacy, and healthcare utilization among adults living with Sickle Cell Disease.

🔗 Repository: [Sickle-Cell](https://github.com/ashsingh2202-web/Sickle-Cell)

---

### Sickle Cell Disease & Healthcare System Navigation

**Methods:** Qualitative Research, NVivo Thematic Analysis

**Focus:** Explored patient experiences with healthcare access, insurance disruptions, and continuity of care.

🔗 Repository: [SCD-Study-IRB](https://github.com/ashsingh2202-web/SCD-Study-IRB)

---

### BRIDGE Scoping Review

**Methods:** Evidence Synthesis, Full-Text Screening, Data Extraction

**Focus:** Examined the role of regional organizations in pandemic prevention, preparedness, response, and resilience (PPPR).

🔗 Repository: [BRIDGE-Scoping-Review](https://github.com/ashsingh2202-web/Scoping-Review)
